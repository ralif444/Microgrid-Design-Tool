# M4a and M4b — equation and unit audit (Doc 9 D-53)

Code: `src/mgengine/m4.py` (model, M4a), `src/mgengine/m4b.py`. Equation numbers refer to the
manuscript `docs/ref/Seminar.pdf` (2.1.2, 4.2.6, 4.2.7; Gambar 4.3a–b, C.4a–b).

## Model (shared)

| Item | Form in code | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| states | δ_i (rad), P_f,i (W), Q_f,i (VAR) per inverter | — |
| algebraic | V_b (V), θ_b (rad) on buses without inverter (A1) | — |
| droop (2.1) on filtered powers | ω_i = ω*_i − m_p,i(P_f,i − P*_i), V_i = V*_i − n_q,i(Q_f,i − Q*_i) | rad/s, V |
| filter (2.7) | dP_f,i/dt = (P_i − P_f,i)/τ_P,i, dQ_f,i/dt = (Q_i − Q_f,i)/τ_Q,i | W/s, VAR/s |
| angles (4.19) | M4a: dδ_j/dt = ω_j − ω_1, j ≥ 2; M4b: dδ_i/dt = ω_i − ω_nom (K66) | rad/s |
| network | S = 3 V̄ conj(Y V̄) (4.7); P_i, Q_i = S at the inverter bus + that bus's load | W, VAR |
| g(x, y) | S_b + S_L,b = 0 on buses without inverter | W, VAR |

The inverter is an ideal source at its bus (A2), so one inverter per bus (K61). The equilibrium
comes from M1: P_f, Q_f from (4.5), δ from the bus angles; it is accepted when the network and
filter residuals are within the M1 mismatch tolerance (else SOLVER_FAILED / EQUILIBRIUM_INVALID).

## M4b (Gambar C.4a–C.4b)

- LSODA (`scipy.integrate.solve_ivp`), rtol 10⁻⁸; algebraic network by Newton at every
  right-hand-side call, started from the last accepted sample with its angles shifted by the
  mean inverter-angle change (keeps the solution on the physical branch).
- Horizon split at the disturbance and at its end when it reverts; the state carries over by
  inverter id, the out-of-service inverter drops out (its bus becomes a load bus).
- A failed integration restarts from the last state with the step limit divided by 4, at most
  eight times; then SOLVER_FAILED / SIMULATION_FAILED, as for non-finite values.
- Samples every min(τ_P, τ_Q)/20 (K69); a disturbance instant is recorded before and after.
- RoCoF = max m_p,i \|dP_f,i/dt\| (rad/s²; adapter divides by 2π).
- System frequency = mean ω_i of in-service inverters (K68).

| Disturbance | Change while active | Magnitude unit |
|---|---|---|
| LOAD_STEP | P, Q of one load + Δp, Δq | W, VAR |
| LOAD_SCALING | P, Q of one load or ALL × ratio | — |
| REFERENCE_STEP | P*, Q*, V* or ω* of one inverter + Δ | W, VAR, V, rad/s |
| INVERTER_OUTAGE | inverter removed, permanent | — |

Classes (K67, K70): network lost (Newton fails, V ≤ 0) or no source left → UNSTABLE. Otherwise,
with settling criteria: not settled in the final window (bands around the final value) →
UNSTABLE; settled and outside the f or V envelope after the disturbance (bands from the design
limits, tolerance (4.11)) → CRITERIA_VIOLATED; else RECOVERED_STABLE. Without settling criteria
no class is set. Configuration checks → INPUT_INVALID / DISTURBANCE_INVALID.
